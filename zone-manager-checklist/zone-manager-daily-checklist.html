How to use this sheet: This is your checklist for your court. Keep it with you and tick each item only after you have personally seen it — opening in the morning, mid-day round, and again at closing. Hand the completed sheet to the Operations Manager by 09:30 AM the next morning. Write NIL where there is nothing to report. A blank box will be read as NOT DONE. Anything serious (fire, injury, theft, food poisoning) — phone the Operations Manager at once, do not wait for this sheet.

1  Opening Readiness Complete before the court opens to customers

  • Main gate, entrance & walkways clear and clean
  • All common seating wiped, aligned, nothing broken
  • Washrooms cleaned, dry, odour-free, fully stocked
  • Dustbins emptied, liners replaced, lids intact
  • Parking swept, markings visible, marshals briefed
  • Court floor mopped, no oil or water spillage
  • All court lighting tested and working
  • Signages clean, lit, aligned, none damaged
  • Landscaping watered, trimmed, no dead plants
  • Umbrellas / canopies opened, clean, no tears
  • Music system on — approved playlist and volume
  • Kids area cleaned, equipment safe, no sharp edges
  • Fire extinguishers in place, pressure OK, in date
  • First aid box present, sealed, stocked, in date
  • All CCTV live, recording, date & time correct
  • My staff in full uniform, groomed, ID cards worn
  • Previous day’s garbage fully lifted from court
  • No pest activity visible in common areas
  • Drinking water and hand wash points working
